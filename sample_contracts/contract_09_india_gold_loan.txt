NPA CLASSIFICATION:
   As per RBI norms, the gold loan account shall be classified as a

DEFAULT AND AUCTION PROCESS:
   If the loan remains in default for 12 months after the due date,
   Muthoot Finance shall have the right to auction the pledged gold
   ornaments to recover the outstanding dues.

   Auction Process (as per RBI guidelines for gold loan NBFCs):
   (a) Muthoot Finance shall send a 15-day advance notice to the Borrower
       via registered post/courier and SMS to the registered mobile number.
   (b) Notice shall specify the outstanding amount, auction date, and
       venue.
   (c) Auction shall be conducted transparently, in the presence of at
       least 2 witnesses, with proper documentation.
   (d) If the auction proceeds exceed the outstanding loan amount
       (principal + interest + costs), the surplus shall be refunded
       to the Borrower within 7 working days.
   (e) If the auction proceeds are insufficient, the Borrower shall
       remain liable for the deficiency.
