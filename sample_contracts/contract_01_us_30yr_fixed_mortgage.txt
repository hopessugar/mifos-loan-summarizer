                    PREPAYMENT AND DEFAULT
===============================================================================

ARTICLE IV - PREPAYMENT

Section 4.01 - The Borrower may prepay this loan in whole or in part at any
time without penalty. There is NO prepayment penalty on this loan.

Section 4.02 - Partial prepayments shall be applied first to accrued interest,
then to outstanding principal, unless Borrower specifically designates the
payment as a principal-only reduction.

ARTICLE V - LATE CHARGES AND DEFAULT

Section 5.01 - Grace Period
A grace period of FIFTEEN (15) calendar days is allowed for each monthly
payment. Payments received after the 16th day of the month shall be considered
late.

Section 5.02 - Late Charge
A late charge of FIVE PERCENT (5%) of the overdue principal and interest
payment, or $168.70, whichever is less, shall be assessed for each late payment.

Section 5.03 - Default
The loan shall be considered in default if:
   (a) Payment is more than 30 days past due;
   (b) Borrower fails to maintain required insurance;
   (c) Borrower fails to pay property taxes when due;
   (d) Borrower commits waste on the property;
   (e) Borrower files for bankruptcy protection;
   (f) Borrower transfers the property without Lender's consent (due-on-sale).

Section 5.04 - Acceleration
Upon default, Lender may, at its option, declare the entire unpaid principal
balance, together with accrued interest, immediately due and payable. Lender
shall provide Borrower with 30 days' written notice before acceleration.

Section 5.05 - Foreclosure
In the event of uncured default, Lender may initiate non-judicial foreclosure
proceedings pursuant to Virginia Code §55.1-1800 et seq. Borrower shall be
liable for all costs of foreclosure, including reasonable attorney's fees.
