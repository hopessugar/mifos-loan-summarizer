                    DEFAULT AND REMEDIES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

EVENTS OF DEFAULT:
(a) Failure to make any payment within 10 days of due date.
(b) Breach of any covenant, representation, or warranty.
(c) Filing of bankruptcy petition by or against Borrower.
(d) Material adverse change in Borrower's financial condition.
(e) Death of a guarantor without replacement guaranty/collateral.
(f) Default on any other obligation to Lender or SBA.
(g) Loss of required licenses (especially OLCC license).

LATE FEE: 5% of the overdue payment, assessed after 10-day grace period.

REMEDIES:
Lender may, upon default:
(1) Declare the entire balance immediately due and payable.
(2) Exercise all rights under the UCC to seize and sell collateral.
(3) Pursue personal guarantors for the full loan balance.
(4) Request SBA to purchase the guaranteed portion of the loan (SBA repair).

SBA LIQUIDATION: If default is not cured, the guaranteed portion may be
submitted to SBA for purchase. SBA may then pursue all available remedies
through the Department of Justice and Treasury Offset Program.
