DEFERMENT AND FORBEARANCE
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

DEFERMENT (interest does NOT accrue on subsidized loans):
   - In-school deferment (automatic while enrolled half-time+)
   - Unemployment deferment (up to 3 years)
   - Economic hardship deferment (up to 3 years)
   - Active military duty deferment
   - Graduate fellowship deferment
   - Cancer treatment deferment

FORBEARANCE (interest accrues on ALL loans):
   - General forbearance: Up to 12 months at a time, 36 months cumulative
   - Mandatory forbearance: Medical/dental residency, AmeriCorps, National
     Guard, qualifying employment under Teacher Loan Forgiveness

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════
DEFAULT CONSEQUENCES
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

A loan enters DEFAULT after 270 days of non-payment. Consequences include:

(1) The entire unpaid balance becomes immediately due (acceleration).
(2) Loss of eligibility for federal student aid (grants, loans, work-study).
(3) Loss of eligibility for deferment, forbearance, and IDR plans.
(4) Loan is assigned to a private collection agency. Collection costs of up
    to 25% of the outstanding balance may be added.
(5) Wage garnishment: Up to 15% of disposable pay (administrative, no court
    order required per 31 U.S.C. § 3720D).
(6) Federal tax refund offset (Treasury Offset Program).
(7) Social Security benefit offset (up to 15%).
(8) Credit bureau reporting — default remains for 7 years.
(9) Professional license denial in some states.

REHABILITATION: Borrower may rehabilitate a defaulted loan by making 9
voluntary, on-time, reasonable monthly payments within a 10-month period.
Rehabilitation removes the default notation from credit reports.

CONSOLIDATION: Defaulted borrowers may also resolve default through Direct
Consolidation Loan with an IDR plan agreement.
